About the game

Earth is dying.
You are the Scout.

Built in C99 with SDL2, The Last Portal is a 2D side-scrolling action-platformer where every level is a planetary trial — combat, environmental puzzles, and scientific quizzes that decide whether a candidate world can sustain humanity.

You are SCOUT — TIER 1, a Field Exploration Agent of the Resistance Science Division. Selected because you are available, capable, and expendable at a level the mission risk profile allows.

Gameplay Systems

01
🚀
Side-Scrolling Combat
Run, jump, double-jump and fire across parallax planet surfaces. Each world hosts unique alien species with their own attack patterns.
02
🧩
Tile-Swap Puzzles
3x3, 4x4, or 5x5 grids — restore the image before the timer expires. Difficulty tiers reward coins, +50% health, or a companion drone.
03
❓
Knowledge Quizzes
Astronomy and C programming questions. Score 75%+ and earn a +50% health bonus on top of per-correct-answer coins.
04
⚛
Companion Drones
Earn a Drone (Level 1) or Cannon (Level 2) by clearing a hard puzzle or winning Connect-4. Auto-targets enemies for 15 seconds.
05
⏱
Race the Corporations
Corporate extraction teams are closing on the nodes. Clear each planetary trial before they seize control of the Engine.
06
👕
Costume Shop
Spend coins on five unlockable suits — each boosts speed, damage, and defense. The Ice Guardian is the strongest in every stat.
Mission archive

The Decline of Earth

"Earth is not dying dramatically.
It is receding — one data point at a time."
— Resistance Science Division, 2338

By 2338, surface habitability outside pressurised zones falls to 34%. Medical oxygen rationing begins in eleven major urban corridors. Migration toward coastal dome clusters exceeds infrastructure tolerance by a factor of six.

You are SCOUT — TIER 1. Field Exploration Agent. Resistance Science Division.

mission_brief_2344.txt
Sec. I — Earth Decline

YEAR 2311. OXYGEN BELOW 17.4%.

Nitrogen-sulphur compounds saturate the lower troposphere. Photosynthetic collapse initiates across equatorial biomes. This is not an event — it is a gradient, slow, measurable, and irreversible by any technology humanity currently holds.

Industrial output does not slow. Population demand does not fall. Carbon sequestration programs arrive forty years too late.

Sec. II — Final Discovery

In 2341, a deep-subsurface survey team beneath the Taklamakan Basin encounters a structural anomaly at 4,200 metres. Isotope analysis places the material at 620,000 years old — predating any confirmed civilisation by an order of magnitude.

Five continental nodes. Identical material. Identical frequency. Each pulses with residual energy on a shared resonance band.

PROJECT CODENAME:
RESONANCE ENGINE

Sec. III — Portal System

The Resonance Engine does not create pathways. It locates them. Natural folds in spacetime exist throughout the galaxy. The Engine detects them and opens a stable traversal window.

In 2344, the first window opens. Terminus confirmed: M-class planet, 43 light-years distant.

  • Oxygen: 21.3%
  • Gravity: 0.94 G
  • Liquid water: confirmed
  • Sapient EM output: none detected

DESIGNATION: CANDIDATE WORLD
— ATLAS PRIME.

Sec. IV — Mission Protocol

Activation requires simultaneous input across all five nodes, in sequence. Partial completion triggers a 72-hour lockdown.

Secondary threat: corporate extraction teams have located three of the five nodes. Their objective is containment — to preserve control over Earth's remaining oxygen infrastructure.

Estimated corporate arrival at Node 4: 38 hours. All five nodes must be cleared within that window.

Sec. V — The Explorer

YOU ARE DESIGNATED:
SCOUT — TIER 1

Selection metrics:

  • Problem-solving under time constraint: 94th pct
  • Field adaptability: above threshold
  • Combat record: functional
  • Psych stability: within acceptable range

You were not selected because you are exceptional. You were selected because you are available, capable, and expendable at a level the mission risk profile allows.

Sec. VI — Planetary Trials

Each node was designed as a test — not a lock to be broken, but a system to be understood:

  • KNOWLEDGE TRIALS — scientific principles
  • SPATIAL TRIALS — reconstruction puzzles
  • GUARDIAN PROTOCOLS — automated defence

THE ENGINE DOES NOT GRANT PASSAGE TO THOSE WHO FORCE IT.
IT GRANTS PASSAGE TO THOSE WHO DEMONSTRATE THEY BELONG.
